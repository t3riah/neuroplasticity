# Neuroplasticity — Your Brain Is Rewiring Itself Right Now

A fun, practical, science-grounded introduction to neuroplasticity: what it is, why it matters for focus, memory, mood, resilience, skill learning, recovery, creativity, and aging — and how surprisingly easy it is to start.

The site includes 13 drills you can do in under two minutes (each with animated hand demonstrations, steps, the simple neurology of why it works, and the benefit it trains), three live "see it for yourself" mini-experiments, a short-form video section with credible creators, and a clear distinction between established findings and emerging research.

**Live site:** https://t3riah.github.io/neuroplasticity/

This repository holds the single self-contained static page (`index.html`) served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root path.
